Single continuous cinematic camera move, no cuts, the camera never pulls back or reverses — it only glides forward. 2D hand-drawn animated film style, flat hand-painted color blocks, bold dark outlines, fine printed halftone dot texture and light film grain, muted grey-green and cement-grey noir palette under cold fluorescent light — the visual style of Liu Jian's animated film "Have a Nice Day".

The camera drifts forward past the restaurant round table and into the pitch-black corridor, the warm bulb fading behind. Cold white fluorescent light grows ahead as the corridor opens into the cluttered aisle of a cheap two-yuan variety store, shelves stacked floor to ceiling with unlabelled plastic toys, lighters, red cords and old watches, the glass counter and a small gilded Buddha pendant on black velvet gradually coming into view at the end of the aisle. A dreamlike passage through memory, continuous forward motion, the surroundings transforming around the moving camera.

Smooth, graceful, slow motion, melancholic and quiet. No people in close-up, no readable text, no captions, no letters, no numbers.
