Single continuous cinematic camera move, no cuts, the camera never pulls back or reverses — it only glides forward. 2D hand-drawn animated film style, flat hand-painted color blocks, bold dark outlines, fine printed halftone dot texture and light film grain, muted grey-green and cement-grey noir palette with sickly fluorescent neon accents — the visual style of Liu Jian's animated film "Have a Nice Day".

The camera continues gliding low over the black river as the orange dusk deepens into night, the red reflection fading from the water. It passes under a dark concrete bridge, and for a moment the frame is swallowed by shadow; emerging on the other side, the riverbank has become a wet township street at night, shimmering with rain puddles that reflect fluorescent yellow-green and faded-red neon signs from pool halls and internet cafes, tangled overhead wires against the dark sky. A dreamlike passage through memory, continuous forward motion, the surroundings transforming around the moving camera.
