Single continuous cinematic camera move, no cuts, the camera never pulls back or reverses — it only glides forward. 2D hand-drawn animated film style, flat hand-painted color blocks, bold dark outlines, fine printed halftone dot texture and light film grain, muted grey-green and cement-grey noir palette — the visual style of Liu Jian's animated film "Have a Nice Day".

The camera keeps gliding forward down the dusty road in pale daylight, then drifts off the road and down an embankment as the sky warms into a sickly orange dusk. Concrete walls and bamboo stumps slide past, the ground cracks open into dry earth, and when the shadow of a roadside tree sweeps over the lens, the cracked black-green polluted river with its garbage-strewn banks opens out below, carrying a red-orange reflection of the sunset toward a hazy distant town. A dreamlike passage through memory, continuous forward motion, the surroundings transforming around the moving camera.

Smooth, graceful, slow motion, melancholic and quiet. No people, no readable text, no captions, no letters, no numbers.
