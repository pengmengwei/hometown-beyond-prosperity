Single continuous cinematic camera move, no cuts, the camera never pulls back or reverses — it only glides forward. 2D hand-drawn animated film style, flat hand-painted color blocks, bold dark outlines, fine printed halftone dot texture and light film grain, muted grey-green and cement-grey noir palette with sickly fluorescent yellow-green accents — the visual style of Liu Jian's animated film "Have a Nice Day".

The camera keeps gliding forward down the wet neon-lit street as the fluorescent reflections thin out into darkness, the shopfronts sliding away behind. It turns gently onto an empty campus road at night where only one streetlamp glows in the distance; as the camera passes through the lamp's pool of light, a small weathered concrete guard booth appears at the roadside, its barred window glowing warm from a bare bulb with moths circling it. A dreamlike passage through memory, continuous forward motion, the surroundings transforming around the moving camera.

Smooth, graceful, slow motion, melancholic and quiet. No people in close-up, no readable text, no captions, no letters, no numbers.
